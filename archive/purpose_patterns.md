## Purpose Types Taxonomy

### Efficiency Purposes
**Signal phrases**: "save time", "automate", "faster", "streamline"

**What user really wants**: Reduce friction, eliminate repetitive work

**Alignment strategy**: Measure success by time/effort saved, keep solution simple

**Common misalignment**: Over-building when simple script suffices

---

### Capability Purposes
**Signal phrases**: "enable", "allow", "make it possible", "add ability"

**What user really wants**: New power they didn't have before

**Alignment strategy**: Verify the new capability actually exists and is usable

**Common misalignment**: Building capability they can't actually use in context

---

### Quality Purposes
**Signal phrases**: "improve", "fix", "more reliable", "reduce errors"

**What user really wants**: Something works better than before

**Alignment strategy**: Measure quality improvement, don't just change things

**Common misalignment**: Making changes that don't actually improve quality

---

### Understanding Purposes
**Signal phrases**: "explain", "how does", "what is", "help me understand"

**What user really wants**: Clarity, knowledge, comprehension

**Alignment strategy**: Verify they actually understand after, not just that info was provided

**Common misalignment**: Information dump without actual understanding transfer

---

### Communication Purposes
**Signal phrases**: "document", "share", "present", "describe"

**What user really wants**: Others will understand something

**Alignment strategy**: Verify the audience could understand the output

**Common misalignment**: Documentation that serves writer not reader

---

### Protection Purposes
**Signal phrases**: "secure", "prevent", "protect", "ensure safety"

**What user really wants**: Risk mitigation, harm prevention

**Alignment strategy**: Verify the threat is actually mitigated

**Common misalignment**: Security theater that doesn't address real risks

---

### Growth Purposes
**Signal phrases**: "scale", "expand", "extend", "grow"

**What user really wants**: Handle more, reach further, do bigger

**Alignment strategy**: Verify growth is actually enabled, not just planned for

**Common misalignment**: Building for scale that will never materialize

---

## Purpose Modifiers

### Urgency Modifier
**Signals**: "quickly", "ASAP", "urgent", "by [deadline]"

**Impact**: Speed over perfection, minimum viable purpose fulfillment

**Risk**: Under-delivering due to time pressure

---

### Importance Modifier
**Signals**: "critical", "important", "essential", "must"

**Impact**: Higher investment warranted, more rigor needed

**Risk**: Over-engineering due to perceived importance

---

### Exploration Modifier
**Signals**: "try", "experiment", "test", "see if"

**Impact**: Minimal investment, reversibility valued

**Risk**: Building permanent solution for exploratory purpose

---

### Foundation Modifier
**Signals**: "will build on", "base for", "starting point"

**Impact**: Invest in extensibility, clean interfaces

**Risk**: Over-engineering for hypothetical future

## Purpose Extraction Signals

### Strong Purpose Indicators

| Signal | Likely Purpose | Confidence |
|--------|---------------|------------|
| "so that I can..." | Direct outcome stated | High |
| "the problem is..." | Pain point revealed | High |
| "currently we..." | Context provided | Medium-High |
| "need to [verb]..." | Action needed | Medium |

### Weak Purpose Indicators

| Signal | Risk | Action |
|--------|------|--------|
| Only technical specs | Purpose may be assumed | Probe deeper |
| Very vague ask | Purpose unclear to user too | Clarify |
| Contradictory signals | Purpose may be conflicted | Resolve |
