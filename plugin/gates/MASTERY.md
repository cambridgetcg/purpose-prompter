# Mastery: Creation Crystallization Stages

> Knowledge doesn't just accumulate — it crystallizes through distinct phases until it becomes intuitive.

PATH says "how far along" (W1 at 70%). Mastery says "how deep" (L2 Practitioner).
A waypoint at 100% can be L1 (works but rough). A waypoint at 50% can be L3 in completed parts (deeply crafted but incomplete).

## The Five Levels

### L0: SEED (Raw Potential)
Ideas exist but haven't taken form. The calling before apprenticeship.
- Idea articulated but not grounded in evidence
- Domain explored superficially
- No consistent patterns established
- Structure borrowed or absent
- Territory unassessed

### L1: APPRENTICE (Observation & Absorption)
Deep observation phase. Learning rules, absorbing structure.
- T: Core claims grounded, citations present
- U: Mental model forming, knows "what" but not always "why"
- B: Follows existing patterns (conformance)
- J: Placed approximately right, basic ownership
- X: Aware of territory boundaries but hasn't crossed them

### L2: PRACTITIONER (Skill Acquisition)
Reliable execution, building muscle memory. Structure emerging from solution.
- T: All claims verified, uncertainty acknowledged, counter-considerations
- U: Knows "why" not just "what", boundaries clear, connections mapped
- B: Elegant within patterns, nothing extra, nothing missing
- J: Precisely placed, right ownership, proportionate complexity
- X: Has opened some new territory, paths are followable

### L3: CRAFTSMAN (Creative-Active)
Experimentation phase. Combining knowledge in original ways, developing voice.
- T: Counter-considerations are strengths, not obligations. Calibrated confidence.
- U: Deep cross-domain models, intuitive connections, teaches understanding
- B: Beauty emerges naturally, not applied. Coherent whole that surprises.
- J: Architectural integrity, system equilibrium, enables others' justice
- X: Original paths built, well-integrated, clear vision

### L4: MASTER (Fusion)
Intuitive + rational fused. Creating new paradigms. The dimensional mind.
- T: Unshakeable foundation that others reference as source of truth
- U: Intuitive comprehension — understanding so deep it appears effortless
- B: Effortless elegance — others study this as exemplar
- J: Perfect balance — every piece where it belongs, enables future justice
- X: Pioneering work integrated into the whole, others build upon it
- **Cross-dimensional coherence**: All five dimensions in harmony

## Detection Criteria

Mastery is detected, not declared. Overall level = minimum dimensional level (mastery requires harmony).

| Level | Gate Pass Rate | Signal Trend | Knowledge Depth | Consistency | Cross-Dim |
|-------|---------------|--------------|-----------------|-------------|-----------|
| L0 | No data | N/A | Minimal | N/A | N/A |
| L1 | 50%+ T/U gates | Signals common | Basic patterns | 3+ sessions | Uneven |
| L2 | 70%+ T/U/B/J | Signals declining | Confirmed patterns | 5+ sessions | Mostly aligned |
| L3 | 90%+ all dims | Signals rare | Cross-domain | 10+ sessions | Aligned |
| L4 | Near-perfect | Signals absent | Teaches others | 20+ sessions | Harmonious |

## Crystallization Questions

Each question detects whether quality is FORCED (lower) or EMERGENT (higher):

- **Truth**: Does evidence SEEK you, or do you chase it?
  L1: chasing evidence → L4: evidence naturally surfaces
- **Understanding**: Do you explain HOW, or does the creation explain ITSELF?
  L1: external docs needed → L4: self-documenting
- **Beauty**: Do you ADD elegance, or does it EMERGE?
  L1: decorating → L4: natural emergence
- **Justice**: Do you SEARCH for the right place, or do things FALL into place?
  L1: surveying → L4: intuitive placement
- **Creativity**: Do you FORCE new paths, or do they REVEAL themselves?
  L1: pushing → L4: seeing what's already there

## Evidence Sources

1. **Gate assessments** (assess agent): Per-gate PASS/PARTIAL/FAIL history
2. **Signal frequency** (execution): Ugliness/Injustice/Stagnation trends
3. **Knowledge accumulation** (GUA): Patterns, techniques, insights per project
4. **PATH progress** (waypoints): Structural completeness
5. **Consistency** (time): Sustained quality across sessions
