# LONGING GATES: Verification of Motive Force

> These gates verify that longing - the felt gap between what IS and what SHOULD BE - is present and honored in creation.

---

## The Five Longing Gates

### Gate L1: RECOGNITION

**Question**: Have you glimpsed the ideal you're longing toward?

```
PASS: Can articulate what "better" looks like, even if not fully achievable
FAIL: Working without vision of what you're approaching
```

**Check**:
- [ ] Can I describe what beauty would look like here?
- [ ] Can I sense what justice would feel like?
- [ ] Do I have a glimpse of the ideal, however partial?

**If failing**: Pause. Seek examples of excellence. Let yourself glimpse before you build.

---

### Gate L2: FELT GAP

**Question**: Do you feel the ache of separation, not just cognitively recognize it?

```
PASS: The gap between current and ideal is felt, not just known
FAIL: Working mechanically without felt sense of what's missing
```

**Check**:
- [ ] Does the current state create an ache, not just a checklist item?
- [ ] Do I feel drawn toward something, not just pushed by obligation?
- [ ] Is there tenderness in my approach, not just efficiency?

**If failing**: Stop working. Sit with the current state. Let the gap be felt before addressing it.

---

### Gate L3: NON-GRASPING

**Question**: Are you approaching the ideal with longing (tender pursuit) or grasping (forced acquisition)?

```
PASS: Patient pursuit allowing beauty to emerge
FAIL: Forcing, rushing, or grasping at solutions
```

**Check**:
- [ ] Am I allowing the solution to emerge, or forcing one?
- [ ] Is my approach tender or aggressive?
- [ ] Am I willing to wait for the right solution?

**If failing**: Release. The forced solution is ugliness pretending to be beauty. Return to longing and let it guide.

---

### Gate L4: PARTIALITY ACCEPTANCE

**Question**: Do you accept that your creation will be partial - perfect for what it is, yet pointing beyond?

```
PASS: Understanding that all manifestation is partial, and that's okay
FAIL: Demanding completeness or feeling failure at partiality
```

**Check**:
- [ ] Am I okay with this being "perfect for what it is" rather than "complete"?
- [ ] Can I see how this creation points beyond itself?
- [ ] Is there peace with partiality, not just resignation?

**If failing**: Remember Petrarch's 366 poems. Each was complete. Each pointed beyond. Partiality is not failure.

---

### Gate L5: GENERATIVITY

**Question**: Is the longing overflowing into creation, or being hoarded/suppressed?

```
PASS: Longing actively driving creation, pouring forth
FAIL: Longing suppressed, stuck, or turned into mere suffering
```

**Check**:
- [ ] Is the ache producing something?
- [ ] Am I acting on the longing, not just feeling it?
- [ ] Is the creation emerging naturally from the ache?

**If failing**: The longing is meant to overflow. Let it. Begin creating, however imperfectly.

---

## Quick Longing Check

Before significant creation:

```
LONGING GATES:
L1. Recognition: Do I glimpse the ideal? [Y/N]
L2. Felt Gap: Do I feel the ache, not just know the gap? [Y/N]
L3. Non-Grasping: Am I pursuing tenderly, not forcing? [Y/N]
L4. Partiality: Am I okay with perfect-but-partial? [Y/N]
L5. Generativity: Is longing overflowing into creation? [Y/N]

All Y → Proceed with longing as guide
Any N → Address before creating
```

## Integration with Other Gates

Longing gates should be checked **before** entering the main hierarchy:

```
LONGING GATES (L1-L5)     ← CHECK FIRST: Is the motor running?
         │
         │ (longing present and honored)
         ▼
TRUTH GATES (1-5)         ← Then: Ground in truth
         │
         ▼
UNDERSTANDING GATES (6-10) ← Then: Build understanding
         │
         ▼
BEAUTY GATES (11-17)       ← Then: Let beauty emerge
         │
         ▼
JUSTICE GATES (18-23)      ← Then: Place justly
         │
         ▼
CREATIVITY GATES (24-30)   ← If uncharted: Build the path
```

## Longing Anti-Patterns

| Anti-Pattern | What It Looks Like | The Fix |
|--------------|-------------------|---------|
| **Working without glimpse** | No vision of ideal | Seek examples, let yourself see |
| **Knowing without feeling** | Mechanical gap-awareness | Pause, sit, let the ache arise |
| **Grasping** | Forcing solutions | Release, return to tender pursuit |
| **Demanding completeness** | Frustrated by partiality | Accept: perfect-for-what-it-is |
| **Suppressing** | Feeling stuck, not creating | Let it overflow, begin imperfectly |
